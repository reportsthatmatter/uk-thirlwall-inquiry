# Thirlwall Inquiry: Report of the Public Inquiry into events at the Countess of Chester Hospital, 2015 to 2018

HC 601, presented to Parliament pursuant to section 26 of the Inquiries Act 2005, ordered by the House of Commons to be printed on 15 September 2026. The report of the Thirlwall Inquiry, chaired by the Rt Hon. Lady Justice Thirlwall DBE, into events at the Countess of Chester Hospital between 2015 and 2018. Three volumes and a summary report. The report names some people by a letter only (Baby A, Mother A, Dr U and so on); the text here keeps every one exactly as printed.

## Scope

The whole report, as the report id `uk-thirlwall-inquiry`, in four volumes of `ingest.ts`, in this order: (1) the **Summary report** (HC 601, 76 PDF pages, 4 chapters, 433 numbered paragraphs), published first on 25 October 2026; (2) **Volume I** (HC 597-I, 248 pages: Foreword; Part One, Chapters 1-13); (3) **Volume II** (HC 597-II, 309 pages: Part One continued, Chapters 14-30); (4) **Volume III** (HC 597-III, 217 pages: Part Two, Chapters 31-44; Part Three, Chapter 45 Recommendations and Appendices 1-9). The Summary stays first so that its paragraph ids, already cited, do not move; the volumes follow in their printed order, as the multi-volume reports here do (Mueller, Leveson): each volume opens with its own title pages and contents, as printed, with no heading added. The Welsh summary, the large-print editions and the Easy Read editions are separate publications and out of scope.

The report concerns the deaths and harm of babies and the experience of their families, a living affected community; see decision 0026 in the site repo. The inquiry's words are served as printed. Lucy Letby's convictions are the subject of a review by the Criminal Cases Review Commission; the report is the Inquiry's, and nothing here is ours to qualify.

## Build

`ingest.ts` declares how the report is turned into Markdown, with the layout passes it composes (each with the page that needed it). Rebuild from the site repo with `pnpm ingest run uk-thirlwall-inquiry`. `golden.yaml` holds fourteen pages (nine of the Summary, five of the volumes) read off the page images or text layer; the known failures each name a bead (see PROCESSING.md). Needs `@rtm/ingest` with the `layoutEndnotes` options and `semicolonWraps` (ingest branch `thirlwallvols-1112`, the release after v0.34.0).
